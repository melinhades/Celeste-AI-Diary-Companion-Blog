# Alex
> refId: Alex

Alex is a minor character in Celeste who is Theo's sister. She does not make any in-game appearances, though Theo describes her as being similar to Madeline in how they "both live so deep inside their own heads". She is renamed to "Maddy" if Madeline is named "Alex", and her name can be changed to anything else by editing the save file.

## Biography

Alex is first subtly mentioned by Theo in Celestial Resort in response to Madeline's refusal to escape the hotel with him. Theo comments that Madeline seems more stubborn than his sister, which he thinks is impossible. Theo also mistakenly calls Madeline by Alex's name when Madeline finds him in the crystal.

At the start of Reflection in a conversation with Madeline, Theo reveals that Madeline reminds him of Alex, who is studying as a civil rights lawyer. Theo remarks that Alex lives deep inside her head and "has this amazing internal compass" so "she'll get where she needs to be". Madeline ends up being amazed by his sister.

In Core, the events of which take place a year after the original story, Granny mentions that Theo has returned to California to support his sister while she finishes law school.

In Celeste 64 in the conversation with Madeline, Theo comments that he hooked her and Alex up so that he can know Maddy's whereabouts anytime. This makes Madeline and Alex dating. Theo also guesses that Alex is in the book that Madeline is writing, and this makes Madeline angry.
