# Prologue 序章
> refId: Prologue

Prologue is the first chapter in Celeste, and starts automatically after creating a save. It introduces the four basic techs within the game: walking, jumping, climbing, and dashing. Three gameplay entities are introduced in this chapter: an Intro Crusher (a block which falls on its own), a collapsing bridge, and the (in)famous intro car. Madeline, Granny, and the Bird are also introduced in this chapter.

Prologue isn't considered an A-Side, but instead an "interlude" chapter, similar to Epilogue. Statistics for the chapter are not recorded in the journal. It consists of 4 screens on the main path, 2 hidden screens and contains no checkpoints. It is longer than Epilogue.

## Plot

Madeline arrives at the foot of Mount Celeste by car and steels herself for the climb ahead. She jumps across several pits, narrowly avoiding a falling block of ice. The Bird is then introduced; it teaches Madeline (and also the player) how to climb a wall vertically. After making it past a few bumpy hills, she arrives near a shack where an elderly woman, later referred to as Granny, lives.

Madeline asks her whether she is on the right trail or not. After the woman confirms this, the mountain climber asks about the collapsing driveway. The old woman starts laughing, claiming that the mountain might be "too much" for Maddy, but she stays determined and states that she'll be fine. Granny then warns her that Celeste Mountain is a strange place, where she might end up seeing crazy things, but Madeline ignores her.

As Madeline steps onto the bridge, it starts collapsing under her feet. Madeline scrambles to cross the falling pieces, while the music intensifies and introduces the "Madeline" leitmotif. The song played is reminiscent of the beginning of Reach for the Summit.

As Madeline leaps over the final gap, the last of the bridge crumbles away, leaving her free falling. As she descends, time slows to a stop, and the bird reappears, teaching the mountain climber how to dash to save herself.

Madeline starts panting as soon as she dashes to the other side. The camera scrolls upwards towards the night sky, and the text "You can do this." appears. Subsequently, the player is returned to the map screen with Chapter 1 unlocked.
